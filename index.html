A little about us

A space for our people

Gamuk is envisioned as a community-focused blog from Kashmir. It brings together local updates, village stories, culture, heritage and reflections in one calm, thoughtful place.

This is an independent publication. Articles and local news should be checked before publishing, and community submissions should be shared with permission.
